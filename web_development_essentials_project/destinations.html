Make the most of your visit

Cox’s Bazar · 3 days

  1. Day 1: Arrive, check in and enjoy the beach sunset.
  2. Day 2: Beach time, local lunch and a relaxed evening.
  3. Day 3: Breakfast, free time and return journey.

Sundarbans · 3 days

  1. Day 1: Travel to the gateway area and overnight stay.
  2. Day 2: Guided boat excursion through permitted waterways.
  3. Day 3: Breakfast and return journey.

Sylhet · 2 days

  1. Day 1: Explore tea gardens and nearby viewpoints.
  2. Day 2: Visit a river landscape and return to Dhaka.

Bandarban · 3 days

  1. Day 1: Arrive and explore the town.
  2. Day 2: Visit an accessible viewpoint with a local guide.

Chattogram · 2 days

  1. Day 1: City heritage walk and local food experience.
  2. Day 2: Explore a local market and return.

Jaflong · 2 days

  1. Day 1: Travel to Sylhet and explore the river valley.
  2. Day 2: Morning sightseeing and return journey.
